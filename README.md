## Notes

- All SVG sources are hand-authored; the icon PNGs are direct raster exports of `awskotlin.svg`.
- The dashed flight trails are replaced by three flowing water lines that spread out of the open box.
- The badge artwork is scaled to 1.12 so it fills the circle with less padding.
- The stroke-only line variant was retired and is no longer part of the set.
- Smaller PNG renders (32/48/64/128 icons, cover PNG) and `favicon.ico` are planned follow-ups.
- The AWS, Kotlin, and Spring marks are simplified vector interpretations used to indicate technology integration; their trademarks belong to their respective owners.
